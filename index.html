Multimodal_AMP_MIC_Prediction: Leveraging Unpaired Data to Mitigate Paired Data Scarcity

Repository → Live Demo →

Formulated and implemented a Biophysical Fusion Ensemble to predict species-conditioned Minimum Inhibitory Concentrations (MIC) of antimicrobial peptides (AMPs). Fused 320-D ESM-2 contextual protein embeddings with 12 handcrafted physicochemical descriptors and learnable bacterial target embeddings across 286 species and 32,607 records. Achieved 0.3625 Test MAE and validated generalizability on 3,497 DRAMP benchmark pairs.

Nucleic_Acid_Binding_Prediction: 1D CNN with Attention vs. Autoencoder Baselines

Repository → Live Demo →

Investigated residue-level RNA and DNA binding interface detection across 1,634 benchmark protein sequences (481,252 residues). Implemented a multi-scale 1D CNN integrated with a 4-head Multi-Head Self-Attention layer and masked Binary Cross-Entropy loss to manage variable sequence lengths (up to 1,781 residues), achieving 0.7340 AUC under severe class imbalance (~9.54% binding sites). Evaluated against an undercomplete convolutional autoencoder baseline.
